About Me

'New vantage point, new perspective' — that's stuck with me since I grew up in a small town, spent a decade in Dhaka, and had a brief stint in Khulna during university. Three years in Copenhagen studying data science, working part-time the whole way through, added another lens. Different cities, different people, different ways of framing a problem — it adds up to a way of seeing things I couldn't have gotten by staying in one place. Off the clock, I'm a lifelong PC gamer — possibly the one problem I've never tried to optimize away. I don't believe in unsolvable problems, just ones I haven't found the right approach to yet.
